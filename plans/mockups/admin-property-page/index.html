Admin property page: five directions

The same sample listing in all five (Two-Bedroom House near SRP Talisay, set to Pending), with a real map and the listing's photos. Open one to scroll and click: photos open full size, the map pans and has a Satellite toggle.

A · Review desk

Photos and the map side by side at equal height. Facts follow, then units, documents and activity. Decision rail on the right.

B · Map first

A large satellite map is the hero, with the photo filmstrip on its bottom edge. Best when the location is what you verify.

C · Tabbed workspace

Overview holds photos, map and facts. Units, documents, activity and reservations each get a tab with a count. Shortest page.

D · Approval checklist

Each thing an approval depends on is a section with a pass or warn mark, with a readiness summary beside the buttons. Best for pending listings.

E · Bento with action bar

Photo and map mosaic, a row of small fact tiles, and the decision pinned to the bottom edge instead of a rail.
